 My story Why I do this work 
 People do their best work when they feel safe and trusted. Building that is why I do this.
 01 Safety was the first lesson
 I studied criminal justice and interned with a domestic violence unit. I learned law enforcement was not my path, but safety stayed with me. I saw how much a safe, trusting environment changes what people can do.
 02 Best Buy taught me engagement
 As a store manager I led teams on the floor and saw how much goes into keeping people engaged. I wanted to keep growing in corporate, where I could act on that passion at scale.
 03 HR is where I build it
 I moved into corporate HR at large brands, then startups, and built the People function at Ridecell over seven years. I want every employee I serve to have the best experience possible.
 I let AI handle the routine work, with a person always reviewing it. That leaves more time for the conversations that matter.
